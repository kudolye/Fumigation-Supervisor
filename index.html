Preparation Readiness Framework

1. Framework Pipeline

CHECK (Site, Equipment, PPE) → IDENTIFY (Gaps & Mismatches) → CLASSIFY (Ready / Not Verified / Not Ready) → DECIDE (Proceed / Hold / Escalate)

2. The Three Decisions
  • Proceed: All applicable readiness requirements are verified, outstanding issues closed, start authorization complete.
  • Hold for Verification: Requirement cannot yet be confirmed; keep operation on hold while obtaining reliable evidence.
  • Do Not Start & Escalate: Critical requirement failed, procedure mandates escalation, or issue exceeds authority.
3. Core Rules of Thumb
  • Both Hold for Verification and Do Not Start & Escalate mean the operation MUST NOT begin.
  • A verbal promise ("we will fix it later" or "I used it yesterday") is NOT verified evidence.
  • A completed check in one domain does not clear the whole job if others remain incomplete.
